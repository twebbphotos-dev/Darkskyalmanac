Take it into the field

Install it before you lose signal

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open app.darkskyalmanac.com in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen

Android

  1. Open app.darkskyalmanac.com in Chrome
  2. Tap Install app when prompted, or use the ⋮ menu
  3. Choose Add to Home screen

Once installed, open it once with signal and it caches everything, so maps, charts and the sky finder work in the canyons with no coverage. Only the live weather needs a connection.
