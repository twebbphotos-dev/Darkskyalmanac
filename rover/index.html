Mars 2020 · Entry, Descent & Landing

The landing · Mission briefing

Atmospheric entry to touchdown takes about seven minutes. One-way light time to Earth is 11 min 22 s: by the time mission control hears the capsule hit the atmosphere, it has already landed or crashed. Everything in between runs on board. Today, that's you.

Welcome to Jezero

2021 · Surface operations

Jezero is a 45 km crater that held a lake about 3.5 billion years ago. A river cut through its western rim and built a delta, now eroded into the cliffs on your horizon. Fine layered sediment like this is where microbial life, if there ever was any, would most likely be preserved.

Rover1,025 kg, six wheels, rocker-bogie suspension MobilityWheels climb blocks up to ~50 cm. Tips over past ~48°; the software stops at 30°. You read 6 outcrops: a current, a quiet lake floor, a stream, a delta building into standing water, groundwater moving through cracks long after, and floods strong enough to move boulders. Six different days in the life of a wet planet.

Every one of them is something a rover has really found. The photographs came from Curiosity in Gale crater and Perseverance in Jezero.
